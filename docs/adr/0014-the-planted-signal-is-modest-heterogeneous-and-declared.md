# The planted signal is modest, heterogeneous, and declared in advance

The synthetic dataset carries a deliberate association between material changes and subsequent claims, sized as a declared design parameter in the data specification rather than emerging from whatever the generator happens to do. Effect sizes differ by change category in a declared ordering. Group comparisons always present both groups with their sample sizes, and all output language is associational.

The tension is real and self-inflicted. Plant no signal and "which changes most frequently precede high-severity claims" — one of the four MVP capabilities — returns noise and demos as a flat chart. Plant a strong one and the product appears to demonstrate that policy changes predict claims, using data fabricated to produce exactly that result. A modest declared signal is the only position that keeps the capability and survives the obvious question, because "how do you know that isn't just your generator?" is answered by "it is, deliberately, and here are the declared effect sizes" — which turns a gotcha into evidence of rigour, provided the disclosure comes first rather than under questioning.

## Consequences

- **The signal is heterogeneous by design.** Capability 3 is a *ranking* question, so a uniform effect across categories produces noise-ordered bars — the no-signal failure mode arriving through the back door. The data spec declares an effect-size ordering (coverage increases most elevated, deductible decreases next, address changes near baseline), giving the portfolio capability a stable answer with an interesting shape and unremarkable magnitudes.
- **Declared effects are CI-validated, not aspirational.** The generator emits from distributions, so realised effects drift. A post-generation check in the regeneration Workflow asserts that measured group effects fall within a stated tolerance of the declared parameters and that the category ranking matches the declared ordering — on every seed and anchor. Without it, a regeneration could silently invert the demo's portfolio chart. This sits alongside the CI execution of every chip: the demo's claims are tested properties of the dataset.
- **"Always show the control group" is enforced where enforcement is possible.** ADR-0007 removed the comparison-aware renderer, so the enforcement points are the Genie instructions (comparison phrasings return both groups with label, rate and n, with example SQL), chip authoring (no chip asks for a lone group's rate, and chips are CI-executed), and the approved-vocabulary list. A typed question asking for one group's rate in isolation will still return one number; that is accepted, and no authored surface models it.
- **One approved-language list covers both boundaries** — associational rather than predictive, and noteworthy rather than accusatory. It governs `pattern_name`, `top_reasons`, UI copy templates and the Genie instruction set alike.
- **Base rates and group sizes are declared too.** A modest relative effect on an implausible base rate still reads as broken to an insurance-literate judge, so baseline claim frequency and the size of both populations at the 90-day default are specified, and `n` is shown beside every rate.
- **The control populations are load-bearing, not decorative** — changed-with-no-claim, claimed-with-no-change, benign gap-window changes, benign pattern instances. They are what make "modest signal" true rather than merely asserted.
- **The disclosure sentence is drafted once and reused everywhere:** *"The dataset is synthetic. Investigation-worthy patterns are deliberately seeded at declared, documented effect sizes — the product demonstrates how historical patterns are surfaced and investigated, not that policy changes predict claims."*
